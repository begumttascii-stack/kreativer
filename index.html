About Us

Youth at the heart of change.

Kreativer Grünen Jugendakademieverein e.V. is a non-profit, non-governmental organisation founded in Germany in 2024.

Based in Pinneberg and Hamburg, our organisation brings together people from different cultural backgrounds and works to create inclusive opportunities for young people.

Our work combines environmental protection and climate action with youth empowerment, democratic participation, human rights, gender equality and social inclusion.

Our vision: Young people who are confident, responsible, environmentally conscious and actively engaged in their communities.
What We Focus On

Building stronger communities through young people.

Our activities connect environmental responsibility with personal development, inclusion and international cooperation.

Environment & Climate

We encourage young people to understand environmental challenges and take action for climate protection and nature.

Youth Empowerment

We support young people in developing confidence, leadership, entrepreneurial, digital and creative skills.

Inclusion & Equality

We create accessible opportunities for migrants, disadvantaged youth, minorities and people with fewer opportunities.

International Cooperation

Through Erasmus+ and international mobility, we connect young people and organisations across Europe.
